My dream girl

“Aap sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho, tum meri dream girl ho.” ❤️

Aap woh insaan ho jise main apni zindagi ke har mod par apne saath dekhna chahta hoon. Main har khushi mein aapka saath rehna chahta hoon, aapke mushkil waqt mein bhi, aapke sapnon mein, aapke dard mein aur zindagi ke un saare chhote-chhote aam palon mein bhi jo hum saath guzar sakte hain.

Main chahta hoon ki hum yunhi saath mein yaadein banate rahein, ek-dusre ke saath aage badhte rahein aur har din phir se ek-dusre ke sath rahein, ek dusre ko aise hi pyaar krte rhe.

Tumhare liye mere dil mein hamesha ek bahut khaas jagah rahegi. Main chahta hoon ki aap hamesha yaad rakho ki aap mere liye kitni khaas ho aur main aapse kitna jyada pyaar karta hoon.

A little prayer

Main dua karta hoon ki aapki zindagi ka yeh naya saal aapke liye khushiyan, sukoon, kaamyabi, barkat aur woh sab kuch lekar aaye jiske liye aap dua karte ho. Allah aapki hifazat kare, tumhe hamesha muskurata rakhe aur tumhare har sapne ko poora karne mein tumhari madad kare.

Aaj tum meri girlfriend ho, kal kya hoga ye Allah jaanta hai, lekin dil se meri hamesha yahi dua rahegi ki ek din tum meri zindagi ki humsafar bano.

Mujhe khudse hamesa sikayat rahegi ki mai mai aap ke sab kuchh nhi kr sakta lekin aapko jo chahiye mai usko pura krne ke liye puri kosish krunga.

“Mera sabse khoobsurat sapna koi perfect zindagi nahi, bas ek aisi zindagi hai jisme tum mere saath ho.” ❤️

Zindagi mein bahut log milte hain, lekin har kisi ke saath woh sukoon nahi milta jo mujhe tumhare saath milta hai. Mere liye pyaar sirf tumhe chahna nahi hai, balki har situation mein tumhara saath dena aur tumhe samajhne ki koshish karna bhi hai. Main perfect nahi hoon aur shayad kabhi ban bhi na paun, lekin tumhare liye behtar insaan banne ki koshish main hamesha karta rahunga.

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki agar tum meri zindagi mein nahi aati, toh shayad mujhe kabhi pata hi nahi chalta ki kisi ek insaan ki presence meri life ko itta achha bana sakta hai.

Main chahta hoon ki aaj ki ye chhoti-chhoti yaadein kal humari sabse khoobsurat kahani ban jaayein.
