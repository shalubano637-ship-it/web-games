689 days • countless memories • one dream

Tum meri zindagi ka itna khoobsurat aur zaroori hissa ban chuki ho ki sach kahun, ab main apna future tumhare bina soch bhi nahi sakta. Jab main in 689 dinon ke baare mein sochta hoon, toh mujhe hamara har pal, har baat, har chhoti si ladaai, har hasi aur hamare saath dekhe hue har sapne yaad aate hain. In sab ke liye dil se thank you yaar. ❤️

Aapne mere har side ko dekha hai, mere achhe bhi aur mere bure bhi, aur phir bhi aap mere saath rahi. Yeh baat mere liye jitni important hai, use main kabhi words mein explain nahi kar sakta.

♡

“Aap sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho, aap meri dream girl ho.”

Aap woh insaan ho jise main apni zindagi ke har mod par apne saath dekhna chahta hoon. Main aapki har khushi mein aapke saath rehna chahta hoon, aapke mushkil waqt mein bhi, aapke sapnon mein, aapke dard mein aur zindagi ke un saare chhote-chhote aam palon mein bhi jo hum saath guzar sakte hain.

Main chahta hoon ki hum yunhi saath mein yaadein banate rahein, ek-doosre ke saath aage badhte rahein aur har din ek-doosre ke saath rahein. Ek-doosre ko aise hi pyaar karte rahein aur chahe waqt kitna bhi badal jaaye, humara ek-doosre ke liye pyaar kabhi na badle.

Tumhare liye mere dil mein hamesha ek bahut khaas jagah rahegi. Main chahta hoon ki aap hamesha yaad rakho ki aap mere liye kitni khaas ho aur main aapse kitna zyada pyaar karta hoon.

Main dua karta hoon ki aapki zindagi ka yeh naya saal aapke liye khushiyan, sukoon, kaamyabi, barkat aur woh sab kuch lekar aaye jiske liye aap dua karti ho. Allah aapki hifazat kare, aapko hamesha muskurata rakhe aur aapke har sapne ko poora karne mein aapki madad kare. 🤍

Mujhe khud se hamesha shikayat rahegi ki main aapke liye sab kuch nahi kar sakta, lekin aapko jo bhi chahiye, use poora karne ke liye main hamesha apni poori koshish karunga. Shayad main har cheez na de paun, lekin aapke liye koshish karna kabhi nahi chhodunga.
